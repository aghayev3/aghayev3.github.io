Every lock gets tested

The Chubb lock's lever.
[full size]

In 1851, at the Great Exhibition in London, an American locksmith named Alfred Hobbs opened a Chubb lock in front of witnesses. It had been sold as unpickable.

Locks got better because someone tested them in public. Your web app works the same way. Someone will test it. It is better if that someone works for you.

Try the most common hole

This is a pretend shop. You are signed in and looking at your own order, number 1041. Change the number and press Submit Query. A well-built app refuses. A lot of real apps do not.

Order Lookup
GET /cgi-bin/orders.cgi?id=1041
Cookie: session=yours

HTTP/1.1 200 OK
{ "order": 1041, "customer": "You" }

Your own order. Now change the number to 1042.
